# Intake interview

Ask in small groups. Accept short, messy, dictated answers. After each group, write what you learned into the project files right away.

## Group 1. Who you are
1. Your name as it should appear on images and posts.
2. Current role and company. May I name the company in posts, or should I write "my company"?
3. Link to your LinkedIn profile.
4. Please drop your CV/resume into `profile/` (PDF or DOCX). Any other documents with your achievements (awards, articles, talks, certificates) help too.
5. Years of experience and the 3–5 results you are proudest of, with numbers if you have them.
→ `profile/background.md`

## Group 2. Why LinkedIn
6. What do you want from LinkedIn: visibility, a new job, clients/consulting, speaking, hiring for your team?
7. Who should read you (roles, industry, country)? Which time zone are most of them in?
8. Which language will you post in?
9. How often can you realistically approve posts? (Default: 2 posts a week, about 1 hour of your time weekly.)
→ `strategy.md`

## Group 3. Topics
10. What do you know better than most people around you?
11. 3–4 themes you want to be known for. (I will propose shares, for example 35/25/20/20.)
12. Any recurring formats you like: interview notes, tool reviews, lessons from projects, life hacks?
→ `strategy.md`, `ideas/idea-bank.md`

## Group 4. Your voice
13. Please put 5–10 texts you wrote yourself into `voice/samples/`: old posts, Slack messages, emails, notes. Dictated voice notes work well too.
14. Anything you never want to sound like (too salesy, too formal, "LinkedIn guru")?
15. Emojis: none, a few (1–3 per post), or more?
→ `voice/voice-guide.md`

## Group 5. Privacy limits
16. What must never appear: employer name, client names, internal numbers, salary, immigration or family details?
17. Interview stories, team stories: OK if anonymized?
→ `CLAUDE.md` (Confidentiality), mark private facts with 🔒 in `profile/background.md`

## Group 6. Look and feel
18. Colors you like or already use (brand, website, videos)? If none, I will propose a palette.
19. Do you have professional photos? Put them in a `Photos/` folder and tell me which you like most.
20. Would you like a short signature line under your name on images, like a personal slogan? If yes, I'll propose 3 options built from your own profile, CV and writing: what you're known for, in your words. You can also keep just your name, or write your own. (Avoid a job title: titles change, the slogan should outlive them.)
21. Do you want memes on some covers? (Light, human posts get more reactions; serious posts stay without.)
→ `brand/visual-style.md`, `brand/photos.md`

## Group 7. Tools and publishing
22. Do you want me to make every post sound human with the humanizer skill? (Recommended.)
23. Will you publish by hand (free) or do you want automation later (paid scheduler)?
24. Do you want a live board (artifact) where you see every post, its status and text?
→ `references/tools.md` check, `references/publishing.md`
